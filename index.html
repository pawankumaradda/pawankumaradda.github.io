How I work

An AI-native product manager

I don't just ship AI features. I run my own product work on AI agents, so I know what they can and can't do before we put them in front of customers.

  • Agents run my routine

    Briefs, pulses, research and competitor watch run on schedules. My time goes to decisions.

  • Prototype before the PRD

    Working prototypes test an idea with customers and engineers before the spec is written.

  • Evidence over opinion

    Deal signals, adoption data and customer conversations feed the roadmap continuously.

  • Human in the loop

    AI drafts, I decide. Nothing reaches customers or leadership without my review.

Guest faculty · HSB, Jaipur · 2026–27

Product Management – I

I teach the core Term I course of the MBA in Product Management: the foundations every PM needs, from the role and product sense to strategy, discovery, requirements, prioritization and roadmaps. Sessions are case-led, using HBS cases such as Google Glass, Motorola Droid 2 and OpenAI's ChatGPT roadmap, and end with each team presenting a full product strategy.
